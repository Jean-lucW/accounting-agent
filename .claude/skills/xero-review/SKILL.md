# Bookkeeping review against the group's rules

Load the `xero` skill first for the API surface, run-code pattern, and safety rules. A review is **read-only**: never fix findings without a separate, confirmed instruction.

## Criteria: load selectively (token efficiency)

`rules/` is the group's own logic, maintained iteratively by the admins, and `docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md` is the generic review method. Do NOT read everything. Load by need:

- **Always** (small): `rules/GROUP.md` (group structure, cost-recognition principles, any group-split or recharge accounts, transaction-type rule), `docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md` (review method + known-issue checklist), and the entity file(s) for the entities in scope only (`rules/entities/<KEY>.md`: roles, revenue posting, critical accounts, tax registrations, payroll specifics; each links on to the sections of the big files relevant to that entity).
- **`rules/EXPENSES.md`**: the entry point for anything expense-coding related. Read the rules (the coding hierarchy where the invoice decides, the per-entity P&L WHITELISTS with no off-list coding without permission, which entity bears staff expenses, and any dated cut-overs in that rule); for whitelists, Grep the specific entity's section instead of reading every entity's; the staff table only when a staff-attributable expense is in play.
- **`rules/SUPPLIERS.md`** (the authoritative supplier to entity to account mapping, one row per supplier family, applied per EXPENSES.md's hierarchy): look up per supplier with `grep -i "^| <name>" rules/SUPPLIERS.md` (or a partial-name grep). Only read it whole when a bulk review genuinely touches 50+ suppliers, where one read beats many greps.
- **`rules/PAYROLL.md`**: only when payroll is in scope (control accounts per entity, journal shapes, master mapping tables); read only the sections for the entity in scope.
- **`rules/INTERCOMPANY.md`**: only when a transaction touches intercompany or group-cost accounts: which flavour carries what between each pair and the strict routing. The pairs and their account codes are `config.intercompany_pairs()`.
- **`docs/INTERCOMPANY_RECON.md`** + the **`xero-interco`** skill: the two-sided reconciliation method and break taxonomy. Every review runs the intercompany reconciliation (the last standard check): a single entity's books cannot reveal an intercompany break, because the error is in the *other* ledger.

**When an admin provides new context about entities, expectations, payroll, or criteria: update the relevant file in `rules/` (append or amend, don't drop existing content) so it persists.** Generic review method that would hold for any group goes in `docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md` instead.

## What a bookkeeping review does not repeat

Balance verification of the standing balance-sheet positions is owned
elsewhere and is out of scope here: prepayments, accruals and deposits by the
standing reports (`report-prepayments`, `report-accruals`,
`report-deposits`, which check the accounts against the Balance Sheet), and
open bills and cross-entity payment routing by the `report-bills` report and
the daily `bill-payments` check. A review finding in those areas notes the
accounting issue (a prepayment never released, a cost that should have been
accrued); it does not rebuild the balance.

## Core review method (from docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md)

1. Consistency with prior months is the primary test: compare each item to the last 2-3 months (further back if needed) of the same supplier/account/pattern.
2. Read the invoice attachment, not just the supplier name, when an item deviates or the supplier has dual treatments (`rules/SUPPLIERS.md` marks them).
3. Benefit principle: cost sits in the entity that benefits, unless `rules/GROUP.md` names an exception (e.g. group-split or recharge accounts held in one entity, where accuracy is critical because errors propagate to every entity).
4. Payroll: monthly totals, line-item to account mapping identical every month, provider fees in the account `rules/PAYROLL.md` names.
5. Tracking: where an entity's rules file requires a tracking category on every cost line, every line carries a correct one.

## Standard checks

Run these for each entity in scope (in addition to the rules/ criteria). Remember: there may be no raw GL feed (`iter_journals` 403s without the journals scope), so work from reports and per-endpoint reads. The entities come from `config.entities()`; get each client with `client_for(e)`.

1. **Trial balance sanity**: `trial_balance(c)`, flatten with `report_to_rows`. Debits equal credits by construction; look instead for: negative balances on accounts that shouldn't have them (negative bank, credit-balance expenses, debit-balance revenue), and unexpectedly large movements vs the prior period.
2. **Unreconciled transactions**: `find_unreconciled(c)` per bank account (`list_bank_accounts(c)`). Flag counts and total value, and anything older than ~30 days. Filter `Status == "AUTHORISED"`: the bank-transaction endpoint returns DELETED/VOIDED rows with `IsReconciled: false`, which read as false positives.
3. **Aged payables/receivables anomalies**: get contacts with balances via `find_contacts`/`c.get_all("Contacts")` (check `Balances`), then `aged_payables(c, contact_id=...)` / `aged_receivables(c, contact_id=...)` for the material ones (contactID is required by Xero). Flag: very old outstanding amounts, negative balances (overpayments sitting unallocated), round-number balances.
4. **Draft/voided journal hygiene**: `get_manual_journals(c, status="DRAFT")`: drafts older than a few weeks are stale (post or delete: ask). Also list recent VOIDED journals (`status="VOIDED"`) as an audit trail check.
5. **Duplicate-looking bills**: `get_bills(c)` over the review window; group by (ContactID, Total, DateString): more than one in a group is a suspected duplicate. Same check on `get_invoices` if in scope. Two qualifications from docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md, both directions:
   - **Never flag the known same-amount patterns**: usage-billing suppliers that issue several same-day identical-amount invoices, and fixed recurring invoices, are normal when the invoice numbers differ. `rules/SUPPLIERS.md` lists the suppliers known to do this.
   - **The real duplicate patterns need the PDFs, not the listing**: a travel-booking platform's invoice plus the merchant's own invoice for the same trip (sharing one trip or booking number), and a card-charge receipt bill plus a bill under the supplier's own invoice number. Confirm by opening both PDFs and comparing the invoice number printed inside; keep the twin matched to the card charge, and propose voiding the unpaid one. A mismatched month on the attachment is not by itself an error: a late-paid invoice may be recognised in the month paid, if `rules/EXPENSES.md` says so.
6. **Suspense/miscoded accounts**: from the trial balance rows, any account named like Suspense/Clearing/Unknown/Ask with a non-zero balance; plus bills/invoices/bank transactions whose lines hit those account codes. **Exception: a by-design clearing account** that `rules/entities/<KEY>.md` names, one that is meant to be filled and emptied by routine postings. Judge it by its balance: a residual persisting past month end is a finding, an in-month balance is not one.
7. **Blank contact / invoice numbers**: bills and invoices with missing `InvoiceNumber` or a placeholder Contact name; bank transactions with no Contact.
8. **Supplier to account consistency sweep**: build the supplier to account map from the prior 2-3 months of bills/bank transactions (line-item AccountCodes per ContactID), then flag current-period lines where a supplier hits a different account; check subscriptions-vs-IT-costs and capitalisation rules from docs/REVIEW_METHOD.md and `rules/EXPENSES.md` on the deviations. Pull the bills/bank-transaction lists once per period and work from the returned LineItems, never a per-document GET loop (daily rate cap).
9. **Transaction-type rule**: every supplier invoice must be a bill; spend money is only for the `rules/GROUP.md` exceptions (payroll control-account payments, lease payments per the lease workbooks, naturally invoice-less items like bank fees, transfers and intercompany cash legs). Flag: spend-money lines to genuine suppliers outside the exceptions, and bills raised for naturally invoice-less items. Small card spend (staff restaurants, taxis, coffee) is invoice-less card spend, an allowed exception where the rules say so: filter it out or the check is all noise. Conversely, where a true purchase has no bill at all (bank line only), note it for the intake ledger, not as a finding: chasing missing invoices is `xero-bills`' job.
10. **Sales tax review**: every tax line in the window against each entity's own rules. The universal rule first: **only tax charged under a registration the booking entity holds is ever recognised as tax**; anything else (foreign VAT or GST, foreign sales tax, tax on an invoice addressed to a different group entity) is part of the cost, folded into the expense or asset line gross. Then per entity, from `rules/entities/<KEY>.md`: which registrations it holds, which tax types and tax accounts each one uses, and the tests that decide them. Generic tests:
    - **Where the supply physically happened decides**, never the tracking category. A domestic supply carries the domestic input type; tax charged in another jurisdiction is recoverable only through a registration there, posted to the account the entity's rules file names for it, and never under the domestic rate.
    - **The seller's tax registration number on the invoice decides, not the seller's name**: marketplaces and multinationals bill from several jurisdictions under one brand.
    - **Every line recognising recoverable tax needs its source document attached**; the journal-line description carries the foreign amount and the rate where the tax was moved by journal.
    - **Service charges and other out-of-scope amounts are never grossed up** as though they carried tax.
    - **A correction moves the tax the document states**, never the domestic tax it displaced.
    - **An entity with no recoverable tax** (no registration): every tax amount is part of the cost, and any tax line in its ledger is a finding.
11. **Tracking**: for every entity whose rules file requires a tracking category on cost lines, every bill/bank-transaction line needs one (FX/gain-loss-type lines excepted); flag missing or implausible allocations.
12. **Revenue shape**: each entity's revenue should have the shape its rules file describes (e.g. an entity whose revenue comes only from an intercompany charge has nothing else; an entity selling outside the group has revenue from its own customers). Revenue of an unexpected kind or in an unexpected entity is a finding.
13. **Quarter-boundary alignment**: where the window spans or approaches a calendar-quarter end, look for costs, accruals and prepayment releases that put an amount in the wrong quarter (`rules/GROUP.md` cost-recognition principles). Known exceptions documented in `rules/` (a usage-billed supplier whose invoice lags the usage month, staff expense claims never split/accrued/prepaid, a conference ticket vs the travel to it) are never findings.
14. **Payroll control accounts**: run `.venv/bin/python scripts/check_payroll_controls.py` (accounts per entity from `config.payroll_controls()`). **Zero is only expected where the corresponding payments have actually been made**: an unpaid payroll bill or a tax-authority remittance not yet gone out leaves a legitimate balance (each control's `residual` in `config/group.toml` says what is legitimate). Cross-check a residual against the bank account it would be paid from (the feed per `rules/PAYROLL.md`'s control-account inventory; server-side: `data/bankfeed/<slug>.md` or the saved statement CSVs for movements up to each account's `csv_until`): money out with the control still open, or no money out and the control already cleared, is the finding, not a balance that simply awaits its payment.
15. **Per-entity account routing**: where the rules give each entity its own account for a cost that exists in several entities (for example a staff benefit or a local payroll tax), check each cost sits on its own entity's account. Findings: the cost on another entity's account; a separately billed invoice in an entity whose equivalent cost is already inside payroll (that double counts the payslip lines).
16. **Known-bad and archived accounts**: match by account name **and** entity, never by code alone: codes are reused across entities, so the same code can mean a different account in each ledger. From `c.get_all("Accounts")` plus the period's postings, check every account `rules/entities/<KEY>.md` lists as never-post, redundant, consolidated, system-only (journals to it are rejected) or archived. A posting to any of these is a finding; an expected-archived account showing live is also a finding.
17. **Dated regime cutovers**: items on the wrong side of a dated rule. Every dated rule in `rules/` (a team or person whose costs move to another entity from a date, a payroll source that changes from bills to the control-account model from a month, a supplier recognised in a different entity from a quarter) is tested here: items dated after the cut-over still recognised the old way, and items before it booked the new way.
18. **Bills missing their source document**: bills in the window with `HasAttachments: false` (bills should carry the invoice/receipt they were raised from; an AUTHORISED bill with nothing attached cannot be verified). Use the list endpoint's flag, not a per-bill `get_attachments` loop; the known exception is intercompany/loan-coded bills whose document lives on the other side's journal.
19. **Intercompany reconciliation (always, whatever the entity scope)**: invoke the **`xero-interco`** skill and run `scripts/interco_recon.py`. Every intercompany balance is held twice, so each pair in `config.intercompany_pairs()` must sum to zero once expressed in one currency; a break is invisible from inside either entity alone. Report every pair touching an entity in scope, classified per `docs/INTERCOMPANY_RECON.md` (one-sided entry / wrong flavour / amount / FX translation / FX on transfer legs / timing / missing mirror account). A zero break still needs a routing check: totals agreeing does not prove each item is in the right flavour (`rules/INTERCOMPANY.md`).
20. **Bank truth test: Xero + our feed = the bank, per account.** The bank is the source of truth (`docs/BANKING.md`), and unreconciled-count checks are blind to anything already marked reconciled, so per bank account test: **Xero's bank-account balance + the unreconciled lines our feed tracks for that account = the actual bank balance at the feed's last-checked stamp**. Xero side: the Bank Summary closing balance (account currency; do not use TB period columns for this). Feed side: `data/bankfeed/<slug>.md` (server; refresh it first with `scripts/bankfeed.py refresh <entity>`), summing the signed lines per account. Bank side: the live API balance **rolled back to the feed's `last checked` stamp** (subtract the completed transactions after the stamp: a live balance carries intraday movements neither Xero nor the feed has seen, and false-flags without this). CSV-only accounts are tested at the statement's closing balance. Match accounts by currency, and read only `current`-type pockets: a bank can hold same-currency merchant or savings accounts that sit in no ledger. Two more findings come free: a bank account with no Xero account at all (its feed sum never nets), and a Xero bank account that no longer exists at the bank but still carries a balance. Any residual after the roll-back is real: Xero postings, our feed, or both are wrong.
21. **GL to Fixed Asset Register tie-out.** For every entity with a FAR: read `GET AssetTypes` (Assets API), map each type's `fixedAssetAccountId` back to its GL cost account, sum the **registered** assets' `purchasePrice` per type (status filter uppercase, `GET Assets?status=REGISTERED`), and compare that total against the GL cost account's **cumulative balance** (the TB's YTD column, not the period movement: the period columns on a BS row show only that period's movement). A gap means the register and the ledger have parted: costs posted to the asset account and never registered, or assets registered at a different cost than posted. Expect part of any gap to be assets still in DRAFT on the register; read those too before calling it. Accumulated depreciation lives in its own contra account and is out of this test's scope.

Budget the rate limit (60/min, 5,000/day **per entity**: a multi-entity sweep parallelises naturally, but per-contact aged reports on a big contact list can be call-heavy; restrict to contacts with non-zero balances).

## Output

One findings table per entity (full entity name, `e.title`, as heading):

| # | Check | Severity | Finding | Evidence (references / amounts / dates) | Suggested action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Severity: high (likely error affecting reported numbers), medium (hygiene/process), low (cosmetic). End with a cross-entity summary: checks run, entities clean, top items needing attention. Accounts are named by name and code together and references are the numbers Xero shows (`docs/COMMS.md`). Anything left with a person goes under `queries` or `manual` and into `scripts/outstanding.py add --domain bookkeeping ...` (a review belongs to the bookkeeping domain). Suggested actions are proposals only: do not execute any write without going back through the `xero` skill's safety rules.
